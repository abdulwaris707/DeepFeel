Frequently Asked Questions

Information regarding our formulations, maceration, and priority delivery.

What makes Extrait de Parfum different from Eau de Parfum?

The difference lies in pure aromatic concentration. Standard Eau de Parfums carry 15%–20% perfume oil, whereas DeepFeel extraits contain 22% to 33% pure aromatic oil. This higher concentration ensures a deeper, smoother dry down that lasts 12 to 16+ hours.

How should I apply my extrait for maximum sillage?

Apply 2 to 3 sprays directly onto warm pulse points: the base of your throat, inner wrists, and behind your ears. Avoid rubbing your wrists together, as friction fractures delicate top notes.

What is your delivery and trial policy?

All flacon orders include a matching 2ml trial vial. You may test the 2ml vial on your skin first. If the scent does not suit your chemistry, return the unopened 50ml or 100ml flacon within 30 days for a full refund.
