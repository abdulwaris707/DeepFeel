Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding shipping, care instructions, and our 30-day guarantee.

Shipping & Delivery

How does complimentary carbon-neutral shipping work? +
All orders over $100 automatically qualify for free standard shipping. We partner with verified carbon offset programs to neutralize 100% of the emissions generated from our studio to your doorstep.
How long will my order take to arrive? +
Standard continental US delivery takes 3 to 5 business days from dispatch. Express priority courier delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. You will receive an email confirmation with active tracking once dispatched.

30-Day Restful At-Home Trial

What is your 30-day return policy? +
We want you to feel the difference in your everyday sanctuary. If any DeepFeel item does not meet your expectations, return it within 30 days of receipt in original unwashed condition for a prompt refund to your original payment method.
How do I initiate an exchange or return? +
Simply contact our client care team at support@deepfeel.com or use our Contact page with your Order Number (e.g. DF-84920). We will provide a pre-paid return shipping label.

Materials & Craft Care

How do I care for the Merino Wool Weighted Throw? +
Because pure merino wool possesses natural lanolin that resists odor and dust, weighted throws rarely need full washing. We recommend seasonal outdoor aeration in shade, spot cleaning with cold water and gentle wool detergent, or eco dry-cleaning.
Are the ceramic pour-over sets dishwasher safe? +
The ceramic dripper and borosilicate glass carafe are 100% dishwasher safe. Please remove the oiled American walnut wooden collar before washing to preserve the natural grain.
