Maceration & Application Ritual

How to Wear & Layer

Apply 2 to 3 sprays directly onto pulse points—the base of the neck, inner wrists, and behind the ears. Because of the high 22%–33% oil concentration, avoid rubbing wrists together as friction breaks down delicate top note molecules.

Storage & Longevity
