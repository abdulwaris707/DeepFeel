THE MAISON CHRONICLE

The Architecture of Memory.

DeepFeel was established to reject the fleeting, synthetic fragrances of mass retail. We create high-concentration extraits de parfum formulated to linger in the air long after you have departed the room.

1. Pure Extrait Concentration

While standard commercial perfumes carry 10%–15% aromatic oils, all DeepFeel master creations are formulated as 22% to 33% pure aromatic concentration. This richness anchors the precious base accords directly to your skin for 12 to 16+ hours.

2. 90-Day Cold Maceration

Each batch of DeepFeel essence rests in temperature-regulated dark stainless steel vats for 90 days. This slow aging allows natural woods, spices, and resins to bond harmoniously without synthetic stabilizers.

"A signature scent is not a cosmetic accessory; it is an invisible signature that announces your presence and preserves your memory."

— Julian Vance, Master Perfumer
