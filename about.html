The Maison Chronicle

Scent is Memory Made Physical.

Founded in 2021, Maison DeepFeel was born out of resistance against the mass-market synthetic perfumes of our era. We create high-concentration extraits de parfum formulated to linger in the air long after you have departed the room.

1. Uncompromising Concentration

While conventional Eau de Toilettes carry a fleeting 8–12% oil ratio, all DeepFeel master creations are formulated strictly as Extrait de Parfum (22% to 33% pure aromatic oil concentration). This richness anchors the heart and base notes directly to the skin, creating effortless 12-to-16 hour sillage.

2. 90-Day Cellar Maceration

Great perfume cannot be rushed on an industrial conveyor belt. Each batch of DeepFeel essence rests in temperature-regulated dark stainless steel vats in our Portland cellar for a minimum of 90 days. This slow aging allows the natural botanicals, spices, and resins to bond harmoniously.

"A signature scent is not a cosmetic accessory; it is an invisible signature that announces your presence and preserves your memory."

— Julian Vance, Master Nose & Founder
Ethical Sourcing

Sustainably Harvested Agarwood & Centifolia Rose

We work exclusively with certified family-owned plantations across Assam, Grasse, and Mysore. For every mature agarwood tree utilized in our Oud Collection, five saplings are planted in partnership with regional reforestation programs.
